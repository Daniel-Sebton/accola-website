<div class="heading">
    <h2>Context</h2>
</div>

•	Renewal and expansion of Spie Batignolles' securitisation programme.

<div class="heading">
    <h2>Objectives</h2>
</div>

- Achieve a target volume of at least EUR300m by extending the programme to unbilled receivables; adding new sellers,
- Manage the effect of seasonality on financing,
- Optimize the price of the programme.



<div class="heading">
    <h2>Methodology</h2>
</div>

•	Phase 1: 
    - Drafting a Request for Proposals
	- Analysis and description of the existing program
	- Estimation of additional volumes that could be mobilized
•	Phase 2: 
    - Consultation with target banks
	- Selection of arranger and lenders
	- Negotiation of mandates and Term Sheet
•	Phase 3: 
    - Transaction execution support
	- Execution of the transaction (review of audits, BUS and documentation)
	


<div class="heading">
    <h2>Results</h2>
</div>

- Programme increased to EUR400m 
- Financing smoothed by modifying the stress factors applied to the calculation of reserves
- Temporary increase in the maximum amount of funding available
- Cost of financing reduced by 20%.
- Addition of a banking partner.
